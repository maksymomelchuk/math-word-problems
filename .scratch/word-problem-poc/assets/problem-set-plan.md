# Problem set plan: types, levels and the 30 slots

Decided in [Which problem types and difficulty ramp does the problem set cover?](../issues/04-problem-types-and-ramp.md) with the parent, 2026-10-03. This file fixes *what* each of the 30 problems trains. The problem texts are written against it later.

## The six problem types

A problem type labels a **relation**, not a whole word problem (see [CONTEXT.md](../../../CONTEXT.md)). The triangle has three relations: "BC на 3,7 см більша за AB" is на, "BC на 5,1 см менша від AC" is на (inverted), and the perimeter is частини і ціле. She names the type of each relation.

| # | Name she learns | Relation | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **На … більше / менше** | A = B + n, A = B − n | «На скільки…?» (n unknown); plain and inverted wording |
| 2 | **У … разів більше / менше** | A = B · k, A = B : k | «У скільки разів…?» (k unknown); удвічі, утричі; plain and inverted wording |
| 3 | **Частини і ціле** | ціле = частина + частина (+ …) | разом / усього, решта / залишилося, perimeter as the sum of the sides |
| 4 | **Дріб від числа** | частина = число · m/n | число за дробом (the number unknown) is the same type |
| 5 | **Три величини** | швидкість · час = відстань; ціна · кількість = вартість; продуктивність · час = робота | any one of the three unknown; unit changes such as 1 год = 60 хв |
| 6 | **Зближення / віддалення** | the gap between two movers changes each hour by v₁ + v₂ (назустріч, у протилежних напрямках) or v₁ − v₂ (навздогін, в одному напрямку) | швидкість зближення / віддалення |

Types 5 and 6 together cover joint motion: a назустріч problem is one зближення relation plus one три величини relation (distance = швидкість зближення · час).

The types fall into three pairs that may share a diagram: 1–2 (comparison bars), 3–4 (a whole split into parts), 5–6 (the three-quantity table). Which diagram each type uses is decided in [What does one guided problem look like, step by step?](../issues/07-guided-problem-flow.md).

## Numbers and wording

- **Whole numbers by default.** The parent: her difficulty is word problems "even with plain numbers", so number difficulty must not mask the reading.
- **Decimals** only with + and −, the only decimal operations she has covered (the parent, 2026-10-03). They appear only in на and частини і ціле relations, only in Levels 3–4, with one or two decimal places.
- **Common fractions** only in дріб від числа relations, with whole-number results. She has covered fraction of a number (the parent, 2026-10-03).
- Every у разів, три величини and зближення division comes out exact, in whole numbers.
- Unit changes are limited to the ones in the school catalog: 1 год = 60 хв, 1 кг = 1000 г, 1 км = 1000 м.
- Problems address her as **«ти»**: «Знайди», «Обчисли», «Визнач». Her workbook uses this form (the parent, 2026-10-03).

## Levels

Each level adds one kind of difficulty, so when she gets stuck the cause is clear.

| Level | Problems | What's new | Numbers | Order |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. One relation | 7 | Each type once, plain wording | Whole | **Grouped**, in the fixed order of the slots below |
| 2. Two relations | 8 | Two relations chained; hidden information (unit changes, an omitted reference, change from a banknote) | Whole | **Mixed** |
| 3. Inverted wording | 8 | непряма форма for на and у разів; число за дробом; the question before the data («…, якщо …»), inside an instruction («Визнач, на скільки…») or in two parts | Whole, then decimal + and − | **Mixed** |
| 4. Chains | 7 | Three or more relations, inverted links inside chains, all six types | Decimal + and − where the shape needs it, whole elsewhere | **Mixed**, ending on the triangle |

**Mixing rules (Levels 2–4):** no two neighbouring problems have the same set of types, and no level name or problem title names a type. Naming the type is only real practice when the order doesn't give it away (Rohrer et al. 2020, in [teaching-methods.md](teaching-methods.md)). Level 1 is grouped on purpose: it introduces the names.

## Notes for later tickets

- **One problem, more than one valid plan.** The walking boy can be split into two три величини relations (3000 : 60, then · 20), or into у разів + три величини (60 : 20 = 3, then 3000 : 3). A problem's relations, and so its types, depend on the plan she picks. The guided flow has to accept either, or settle on one canonical plan per problem.
