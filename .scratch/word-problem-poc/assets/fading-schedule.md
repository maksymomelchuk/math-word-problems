# How the guidance fades across the problem set

Decided in [How does the guidance fade across the problem set?](../issues/08-guidance-fading.md) with the parent, 2026-10-03, on revisiting. It replaces the first schedule, [fading-schedule-v1.md](fading-schedule-v1.md), which ended with her writing whole problems alone in the notebook. The parent objected: "it should be better way to decompose that problem and solve it step by step." Terms are from [CONTEXT.md](../../../CONTEXT.md). The fully guided problem it starts from is in [guided-flow.md](guided-flow.md); the levels and slots are in [problem-set-plan.md](problem-set-plan.md).

The parent made two calls: the notebook stays, and a solo try is offered at 4.6–4.7. Everything else was settled by app practice, research and how her school does it, as the parent asked:

- [fading-revisit-apps.md](fading-revisit-apps.md) covers the apps.
- [fading-revisit-evidence.md](fading-revisit-evidence.md) covers the research.
- [fading-revisit-school.md](fading-revisit-school.md) covers Ukrainian school.
- [fading-revisit-proposal.md](fading-revisit-proposal.md) was the starting point (its option B).
- One extra source, on common-wrong-answer feedback in ASSISTments, is cited where it's used.

## In short

- **No problem is handed over whole.** Every problem is a sequence of steps up to 4.7, one per screen, and the app checks each step before the next one opens. The one exception is the solo try, which is her choice.
- **What fades is what the app tells her, not what it checks.** Steps move from prompted menus to her notebook on a fixed schedule, as before. But every faded step is still checked: by a typed result or a pick where the app can, and by yes/no questions beside the model where it can't. Keeping step checks to the end showed no harm in any study found; step-checking tutors beat no tutoring by d ≈ 0.76 (VanLehn 2011). No product surveyed stops checking steps on a schedule.
- **From 3.1 she makes the plan herself, one action at a time, as her school does.** «Про що дізнаєшся першою дією?» She writes it, the app checks what she wrote, then she computes it and types the result. Once she gets two plans in a row right, she writes the whole plan first. After a miss she's back to one action at a time.
- **Every action's result is typed and checked, from 2.1 to 4.7.** A wrong result that a wrong sign would give gets that sign's hint.
- **The direction check stays when the actions go to paper**, before «у … разів» and rate actions. It fades for each relation type once she gets it right twice in a row.
- **From 4.4 she chooses the next step herself.** That replaces the checklist card, which is gone.
- **Unaided work is her homework, plus the optional solo try** at 4.6, 4.7 and replays after the set.

## The schedule

|---|---|---|---|---|

| **2.1–2.4** | Перекажи, Знайти, Відомо, Порівняння, Тип і схема, План | Обчисли, Відповідь | Plan cards. Then for each action the app names it, she writes it in the notebook and types its result | The same |
| **2.5–2.8** | The same | The same | The same | **Slot count hidden** |
| **3.1–3.8** | Перекажи, Знайти, Порівняння, Тип і схема | **+ Відомо (the short record), План**, Обчисли, Відповідь | **Her own plan**, in small or big steps (below), each line checked by a pick, each result typed | Relations listed; **she picks each relation's diagram and places the «?»**; «Чому?» only after the decode |
| **4.1–4.3** | Порівняння only (4.1, 4.2; 4.3 has no comparison, so nothing is prompted) | Everything else, in routine order. **The diagram too** | The same | «Чому?» after the decode (4.1, 4.2) |
| **4.4–4.5** | Nothing | Every step. **She picks each next step** | The same | — |
| **4.6–4.7** | Nothing | The same, **or the solo try**, if she chooses it | The same | — |

- **Order in Level 3.** Перекажи → Знайти → Порівняння → the short record on paper → Тип і схема → the plan and actions on paper → Відповідь. The decode comes before she writes the record, because the record holds the restated comparison. The restated sentence stays on screen for her to write in.
- **The stage boundaries and handover points are unchanged from v1**, and so are the reasons: the actions go to paper first, the short record and the plan at 3.1, and the decode is the last step the app asks. There's no big jump on the hardest problems.
- **Why the prompts still fade on a fixed schedule.** Fading on her success would need full guided menus for every problem she might be held on, which is double the data. Fixed fading did no better than no fading in one study (Salden et al. 2010), so the checks below adapt to her instead, which costs no data. This is a limit (see Limits).
- **Steps a problem doesn't have stay skipped**, as in the guided flow.

## Plan and actions from 3.1

This is the core of the change. School breaks a compound problem down by asking, one action at a time, «Що можна знайти на підставі цих даних? Якою дією?», and its plan is the numbered actions. Writing a plan skeleton before computing isn't a school habit at grade 5–6 ([fading-revisit-school.md](fading-revisit-school.md)). Line-by-line checks suit learners new to planning best. Checking the whole plan after she's written it suits her once she gets lines right. Учи.ру's stated rule is the same: bigger steps as the child improves, smaller steps after a mistake.

**Small steps.** This is where she starts at 3.1, and where she goes back to after a miss. For each action:

1. «Про що дізнаєшся першою дією? Запиши в зошит «1)» і після риски — що знаходиш.» She writes the explanation and leaves the calculation for later. «Готово».
2. «Що знаходить твоя перша дія?» The options are:
   - every quantity a valid plan can find at this point, given her earlier actions, from the problem's plans and `Order:` lines;
   - the asked quantity, if it can't be found yet (the common jump to the answer);
   - «Інше».

   Every valid plan is accepted.
3. The direction check, if this action has one and it's still active (below).
4. «Обчисли і допиши дію. Введи результат.» She completes «1) 8,4 + 3,7 = 12,1 (см) — довжина сторони BC;» and types 12,1.
5. Next action. After the action that finds the asked quantity: Відповідь.

**Big steps.** These come after two problems in a row in which every plan line was right first time.

1. «Запиши план: про що дізнаєшся кожною дією.» She writes the numbered explanations.
2. «Скільки дій у твоєму плані?» on the keypad. It's right if it matches any valid plan.
3. The same line picks, one after another.
4. The actions one at a time, as in small steps 3–4.

A plan line that isn't right first time sends the next problem back to small steps. Results don't move her between small and big steps. They're about computing, not planning.

**Level 2 is unchanged in shape.** The plan is still her pick of cards. For each action the app names it («Дія 2: відстань, яку хлопчик пройде за 20 хв»), asks the direction check if there is one, and she writes and types it.

## What happens on a miss

- **A wrong plan line** («Інше», the asked quantity too early, or a wrong action count). First the school's question as a hint: «Що можна знайти з того, що вже відомо? Подивись на короткий запис.» Then the valid line or lines, with «Виправ у зошиті». She carries on from a valid line, and it's recorded against that line.
- **A wrong result.**
  - If the result is what a wrong sign gives with the same numbers (3000 · 3 = 9000, 3000 + 3 = 3003, or a swapped order for − and :), she gets that sign's hint from the data.
  - Otherwise, she gets «Не сходиться» and the action's hint.
  - A second miss shows the action with its reason. If the sign wasn't inferred, she then sees «У тебе така сама дія?» with «Так, помилка в обчисленні» / «Ні, інша дія», which keeps slips apart from wrong actions. Then «Виправ у зошиті».
  - Matching a wrong answer to its likely mistake is ASSISTments' common-wrong-answer feedback. In a randomized study it raised correctness on the next problem of the same skill, and short feedback did better than long ([Gurung et al.](https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10417271), seen in a summary).
- **Prompted steps** keep hint-then-show, as in the guided flow.
- **A paper step's «Ні»**: «Виправ у зошиті», then «Далі».

## The direction check

From [Help her pick the operation in Обчисли](../issues/16-help-pick-operation.md): «Спершу подумай: За 20 хв він пройде більше чи менше, ніж 3000 м?» with «Більше» / «Менше», hint-then-show, and its reason. School teaches the same thing as прикидка from grade 1. It asks it before the analysis on this very type of problem ([fading-revisit-school.md](fading-revisit-school.md)).

- **Where**: before actions the data marks. These are «у … разів» and rate actions (Три величини, Зближення) whose result is bigger or smaller than a number she already knows.
- **Never on «дріб від числа»**, or any multiplier below 1. Children choose × or : by the size they expect, and there "less" goes with multiplying (3/5 of 40 is 40 · 3/5). That is the documented error, so the check would teach it ([fading-revisit-evidence.md](fading-revisit-evidence.md), question 5).
- **On paper too**: it runs before she writes the action. In Level 2 that's after the app names the action. From 3.1 it's after her plan line is checked.
- **It fades per relation type.** After two right in a row on a type, it stops for that type. Each one only counts if the action that follows is right too, since with two buttons a right answer is weak evidence. A wrong sign on that type brings it back. Once it has faded, «Підказка» on the action still asks it.

## Paper steps

- **One step at a time, always.** The heading appears (for example «Запиши короткий запис»). She writes that part in her notebook and taps «Готово». The app then checks it (below) before the next step opens. There is no whole-problem mode outside the solo try.
- **Only her paper steps go in the notebook.** She doesn't copy the parts that are still prompted.
- **Перекажи has no line in the write-up**, so from 4.1 it's only a heading, «Перекажи собі задачу», with «Готово».
- **Paper, not a stylus.** Only the tablet has a stylus, and the app couldn't check a drawing anyway.

## How each paper part is checked

The app checks what it can. Self-checks are kept only where it can't, because children overrate their own work ([fading-evidence.md](fading-evidence.md), question 3).

| Part | Checked by | From |
|---|---|---|
| Actions | Each result, typed | 2.1 |
| Plan | A pick per line; in big steps, the action count first | 3.1 |
| Short record | The model, with «Шукане — ⟨the «?» line⟩?» and «Біля кожного числа є одиниця?»; for each comparison, «Порівняння записане від шуканого: «⟨the restated line⟩»?» | 3.1 |
| Decode, once on paper | For each comparison, «Хто більший: ⟨X⟩ чи ⟨Y⟩?», a pick, before the short record's model | 4.4 |
| Diagram | «Яка схема в тебе?», a pick among the type sketches; then the model and ««?» стоїть там, де шукане?» | 4.1 |
| Answer | The model, with «Відповідь — повним реченням про ⟨the asked quantity⟩?» | 2.1 |
| Next step | «Який крок далі?», a pick | 4.4 |

- **Picks and self-checks are templates.** They're filled in from the write-up and the plans, so nothing is written per problem beyond the tags on the short record's «?» line and its restated comparison lines.
- **Her last result is the final answer**, so there's no separate final-answer entry, and no "where did yours go off" tap: the app knows which action went wrong. For 3.7, the name part is a pick between the two names. 3.3 has two parts, each a result.

## Choosing the next step (4.4–4.7)

Before each step: «Який крок далі?», with the eight step names as buttons.

- **A wrong pick**: «Спершу — ⟨step⟩.», and that step opens.
- **A step the problem doesn't have**: «Тут немає порівняння. Який крок далі?»
- **In small steps**, picking План runs the plan and the actions together.
- **It's free, recorded, and needs no data.** It hands over what the checklist card was for: the routine's order, without being told.
- **It starts at 4.4, not 4.6.** A memorised routine fades without review, and some 6th graders never mastered a 7-step one (Montague). So she gets four problems and their repeats, not two.

## The solo try (4.6, 4.7, and replays after 4.7)

The problem opens with «Крок за кроком» (the default) and «Спробую сама».

- **Solo**: the problem text and the notebook. She writes the whole write-up and types the final answer. «Розбий на кроки» is there at any time.
- **A right answer** goes to the ordinary end screen, with the Розбір and the self-checks for the short record and the answer.
- **A wrong answer**: «Не сходиться. Розберімо крок за кроком.» The step-by-step flow then runs from the first step at her current stage, and its checks find where hers went off.
- **«Розбий на кроки»** switches to the step-by-step flow from the first step, too. She taps «Готово» on the parts she has already written.
- **Recorded**: which she chose, and whether and where she switched.
- **Why offered, not imposed**: the parent didn't want her left alone with a whole problem. The whole problem with step-by-step help on request is the most common ending in the apps surveyed (ASSISTments, Zearn's Boost, Khan, Учи.ру). An unaided try was worth keeping as an option ([fading-revisit-evidence.md](fading-revisit-evidence.md), question 1). It also gives the parent a moment to watch her work alone.

## Missed problems

Unchanged from v1, except for what counts as missed.

- **A problem is missed** when the app had to show a plan line or an action's result.
- It comes back once, at the end of its level (after 4.7 for Level 4), at her current stage and step size. It doesn't come back a third time.
- Inside a problem, help comes at once, as above.

## Hints

- **Prompted steps** keep the guided flow's hints, which come after a wrong answer. There's no hint button.
- **Paper steps get «Підказка».** The first tap shows the step's self-question, the second shows the model (for a plan line, the valid lines). The self-questions are the old card's lines, plus these:
  - **План**: the school's chain from the question, «Що потрібно знати, щоб знайти ⟨the asked quantity⟩? Що з цього вже відомо?»
  - **Обчисли**: the direction question if the action has one, otherwise «Що означає кожне число в дії?»
- **Hints are free and recorded.** Asking for help never costs more than guessing.

## Built-in hints

As in v1:

| Hint | How it fades |

| Slot count in План | Shown in Level 1 and 2.1–2.4. Hidden in 2.5–2.8. Gone from 3.1, where she writes the plan |
| Relations listed in Тип і схема | Listed while Тип і схема is prompted (Levels 1–3) |
| Diagram chosen for her | Levels 1–2. From 3.1 she picks each relation's diagram. From 4.1 she draws it and picks which one she drew |

Then the new thing at 3.1 is only placing the «?», and the sketches double as the options for «Яка схема в тебе?».

## Replays

- **A replayed problem runs at her current stage and step size.** It prompts only the steps that both the replayed problem and her furthest problem prompt, as in v1, so it needs no extra data.
- **After 4.7**, every replay is all paper, she picks the next step, and the solo try is offered.

## Recorded per step

Besides the guided steps' hints, shown answers and slips, the records hold:

- each plan-line pick, and each move between small and big steps;
- each typed result, with the sign inferred from a wrong one;
- each direction-check answer, and when the check faded or came back for a type;
- each next-step pick;
- each self-check answer and each «Підказка» tap;
- the solo-try choice and where it switched.

## What each problem needs in the data

| Problems | Guided-step data |
|---|---|
| 1.1–1.7 | Everything in "What each problem needs in the data" in [guided-flow.md](guided-flow.md) |
| 2.1–2.8 | The same, except the Відповідь menu |
| 3.1–3.8 | Text, Retell, Asked, Decode, «Чому?» (after the decode only), Relations, Diagram |
| 4.1–4.2 | Text with comparison spans, Decode, «Чому?» |
| 4.3–4.7 | None |

**All 30 also need:**

- the write-up, with the short record's «?» line and restated comparison lines tagged;
- the other plans, with their `Order:` lines;
- the final answer (3.7's two names for the name pick);
- the closing screen's checks and key idea;
- **for every action, in every valid plan:**
  - its hint;
  - a sign hint for each wrong sign where a wrong sign is a likely mistake (у разів, rates, inverted wording, as in [16](../issues/16-help-pick-operation.md));
  - a direction check where one applies, with the relation type it belongs to.

**Derived by script, not written:** the valid lines at each point of a plan, the asked-quantity option, the action count, «Хто більший?», and the wrong-sign results.

**No longer needed:** the checklist card's wording, and the self-checks for the plan, actions and diagram.

So 15 problems still need full or nearly full guided data, 10 partial and 5 none, as before. On top come about 57 action hints for 3.1–4.7 (42 in the main plans, about 15 in the other plans), plus sign hints and direction checks where they apply.

## Not chosen

- **The whole problem at once in 4.4–4.7** (v1). The parent objected to it.
- **Everything on screen to the end, with word tiles** (the proposal's A). The parent kept the notebook. She'd never write a plan, and it doubles the data.
- **Writing each step, then entering it in menus** (the proposal's C). Every step is done twice, and it needs full menus for all 30.
- **The app naming every action to the end** (Level 2's mechanic everywhere). She'd never make a plan, and the plan is what the PoC trains.
- **The whole plan first, always.** It isn't a school habit, and line-by-line checks suit a learner new to planning. It comes as the big step once she's ready.
- **A fixed switch to the whole plan at 4.1.** It moves on her plan lines instead, at no data cost.
- **Adaptive fading of the prompted steps.** It needs full menus for every problem. The checks adapt instead.
- **The direction check to 4.7 regardless**, or as a rule for the sign. It fades per type once she's right, and it never appears where "less" doesn't mean "divide".
- **Choosing the next step only in 4.6–4.7.** Too few repetitions to stick.
- **The checklist card.** Choosing the next step hands over the order, and its lines live on behind «Підказка».
- **A solo try imposed, or none at all.** The parent chose it as an option.

## Limits

- **Not tried with her.** She found 2.3 hard even fully guided. The schedule is still a guess, and its evidence is weak to moderate, mostly from other ages and tasks.
- **The prompts still fade on a fixed schedule.** Adaptive fading beat fixed fading in the one study comparing them. Here only the checks adapt.
- **Picks after writing trust her.** She can tap a valid line she didn't write. The asked-quantity option and «Інше» make an honest answer easy, and the parent reading her notebook is the backstop.
- **The short record and the answer sentence are still her own judgement**, with the model and item-by-item checks.
- **No unaided problem unless she chooses the solo try.** Her homework is the unaided stage, and the PoC doesn't see it.
- **Problems are longer.** The triangle may take about 8–11 minutes step by step (a guess). Products for her age plan sessions of 15–30 minutes ([fading-revisit-apps.md](fading-revisit-apps.md), question 7), so plan on 1–2 Level 4 problems per sitting.
- **The direction check's own benefit is untested.** Only the habit it targets is well documented.
