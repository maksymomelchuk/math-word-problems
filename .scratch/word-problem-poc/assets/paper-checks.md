# Paper checks A and B

Written for [Write the two paper checks](../issues/05-write-paper-checks.md). Check A is solved before she sees the PoC, and check B after it. Comparing the two gives the *does it help* half of the **verdict** (see [CONTEXT.md](../../../CONTEXT.md)).

- Her printable sheets: [paper-check-a.html](paper-check-a.html) and [paper-check-b.html](paper-check-b.html). Open one in a browser and print it on A4. Each sheet is two pages, with a squared box under each problem.
- This file holds everything for the parent: the pairs, the rules for running a check, the worked answers, the scoring guide and the score sheet.

This file is the source. If a problem changes here, change it on her sheet too.

## The four pairs

Each problem in B matches the problem in A with the same number: the same relations in the same order, the same wording, the same level and the same number kinds. Only the story and the numbers change.

| # | Relations (level) | What it tests | Check A | Check B |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | на + частини і ціле (Level 2) | The reference is left out («а на другій — на 8 менше»), and the question asks for the total, not the second amount | Books on two shelves | Apples in two baskets |
| 2 | три величини + три величини (Level 2) | A variant of the walking boy: the hour must become 60 minutes | A grandmother walking | A skier |
| 3 | у разів (*inverted*) + частини і ціле (Level 3) | «…, що в 3 рази більше, ніж у другому»: the word says *більше*, but she has to divide | Potatoes in two sacks | Pencils in two boxes |
| 4 | на + на (*inverted*) + частини і ціле (Level 4) | A variant of the triangle: a chain of three relations, decimal + only, with the "less than … by" link inside it | Triangle MNK | Triangle DEF |

What the ticket asked each check to contain:

- **"Less than … by" problem**: problem 4. Problem 3 adds a second inverted relation, of the у разів type and the opposite direction, so that the Decode score doesn't rest on a single problem.
- **Rate problem**: problem 2.
- **Multi-step problem**: problem 4, with three steps. The other three problems have two steps each.

Number rules from [problem-set-plan.md](problem-set-plan.md): whole numbers in problems 1–3. Decimals appear only in problem 4, only in на and частини і ціле relations, and only with +. Every division comes out exact. The only unit change is 1 год = 60 хв. Every problem uses the «ти» form.

## Running a check

The rules are the same for A and B, so that the two results can be compared.

1. **Setting.** One sitting at a table, with the printed sheet and a pen. No calculator, as in class. There's no time limit, but note how long she takes.
2. **What to say.** Say the same thing both times: «Це не контрольна, оцінки не буде. Розв'язуй, як умієш: мені важливо побачити, як ти думаєш.»
3. **No help.** Don't read the problems aloud, give hints, or answer "is this right?". If she asks what an everyday word means (what a мішок is), tell her. If she asks anything about the maths, say «Роби так, як розумієш». Write down every question she asks.
4. **Skipping.** She may skip a problem and come back to it later.
5. **Missing explanations.** When she's done, point at each step that has no explanation after the dash and ask «Що ти знайшла цією дією?». Write her answer next to it in another colour. She doesn't change anything on the sheet. If she wrote an expression instead of steps, ask what each part of it means.
6. **One question for her.** Ask «Яка задача була найважча? Чому?» and write down her answer.
7. **After check A.** Don't go over the solutions with her, and don't show her the score, until check B is done. Going over check A would teach her the same four pairs that check B re-tests, outside the PoC. If she asks, tell her you'll look at it together after the game.

## Scoring

Score each problem on four criteria. The first three measure *understanding*, which is what the PoC trains. The fourth is the final answer, which also depends on arithmetic.

| Criterion | Points | What earns the points |
|---|---|---|
| **Asked** | 0–1 | Her answer, or her last step, is the quantity the question asks for, in a fitting unit. The number may be wrong. |
| **Plan** | 0–2 | **2**: she finds every quantity the route needs, in an order where each step's inputs are already known, and ends on the asked quantity. **1**: part of the route: she finds at least one needed quantity, but stops early, skips a needed one, or adds a step that leads nowhere. **0**: no needed quantity, for example the given numbers combined in one step, or nothing to judge. |
| **Decode** | 0–1 | She reads the problem's one tricky relation correctly: the right operation *and* the right reference. Each problem's tricky relation is listed in the guide below. |
| **Answer** | 0–1 | The final number is right. A missing unit is noted but not penalised; a wrong unit scores 0. |

How to apply the criteria:

- **Read the plan from her explanations**, the text after each dash. Any route a teacher would accept counts, including an expression such as «3600 : 60 · 10».
- **Plan judges the route, not how she read the tricky relation.** If she finds the right quantities in the right order but reads the tricky relation wrong, she gets Plan 2 and Decode 0. That way the score shows whether she can plan but misreads, or can't plan yet.
- **A criterion she didn't reach scores 0.** For example, if she stops before the tricky relation, Decode is 0.
- **Don't penalise a missing short record or diagram.** Teachers differ on it. Note whether she made one.
- **An arithmetic slip on its own** costs only the Answer point.

Understanding per problem is Asked + Plan + Decode, out of 4, so one check scores out of 16. The Answer total is out of 4.

## Worked answers and scoring guide, by pair

Each write-up follows the school format from [ukrainian-grade6-word-problems.md](ukrainian-grade6-word-problems.md): an optional short record, then «Розв'язання» by numbered steps, then «Відповідь».

### Pair 1: на + частини і ціле, with the reference left out

**A.** «На першій полиці 26 книжок, а на другій — на 8 книжок менше. Скільки книжок на обох полицях?»

```
I полиця — 26 кн.
II полиця — ?, на 8 кн. менше
Разом — ?

Розв'язання
1) 26 − 8 = 18 (кн.) — на другій полиці;
2) 26 + 18 = 44 (кн.) — на обох полицях.
Відповідь: 44 книжки.
```

**B.** «У першому кошику 34 яблука, а в другому — на 9 яблук менше. Скільки яблук в обох кошиках?»

```
I кошик — 34 ябл.
II кошик — ?, на 9 ябл. менше
Разом — ?

Розв'язання
1) 34 − 9 = 25 (ябл.) — у другому кошику;
2) 34 + 25 = 59 (ябл.) — в обох кошиках.
Відповідь: 59 яблук.
```

**Tricky relation:** the second amount. «На 8 менше» is compared with the first shelf, which the sentence doesn't name again, so she must work out 26 − 8.

| Her answer (A / B) | What happened | Asked | Plan | Decode |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 44 / 59 | Right | 1 | 2 | 1 |
| 18 / 25 | Found the second amount and stopped | 0 | 1 | 1 |
| 60 / 77 | Added 8 for the second amount, then found the total | 1 | 2 | 0 |
| 34 / 43 | Added the two given numbers in one step | 1 | 0 | 0 |

### Pair 2: три величини + три величини, with a unit change (the walking boy)

**A.** «Бабуся пройшла 3600 м за годину. Яку відстань вона пройде за 10 хвилин?»

```
За 1 год (60 хв) — 3600 м
За 10 хв — ? м

Розв'язання
1 год = 60 хв
1) 3600 : 60 = 60 (м) — проходить бабуся за 1 хв;
2) 60 · 10 = 600 (м) — пройде бабуся за 10 хв.
Відповідь: 600 м.
```

Other accepted plans: «1) 60 : 10 = 6 (разів) — у стільки разів 10 хв менше, ніж 1 год; 2) 3600 : 6 = 600 (м).», or the expression «3600 : 60 · 10 = 600 (м)».

**B.** «Лижниця пройшла 6000 м за годину. Яку відстань вона пройде за 15 хвилин?»

```
За 1 год (60 хв) — 6000 м
За 15 хв — ? м

Розв'язання
1 год = 60 хв
1) 6000 : 60 = 100 (м) — проходить лижниця за 1 хв;
2) 100 · 15 = 1500 (м) — пройде лижниця за 15 хв.
Відповідь: 1500 м.
```

Other accepted plans: «1) 60 : 15 = 4 (рази); 2) 6000 : 4 = 1500 (м).», or «6000 : 60 · 15 = 1500 (м)».

**Tricky relation:** the unit change, 1 год = 60 хв. Evidence that she read it: she wrote «1 год = 60 хв», divided by 60, or worked out 60 : 10 (60 : 15).

| Her answer (A / B) | What happened | Asked | Plan | Decode |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 600 / 1500 | Right | 1 | 2 | 1 |
| 60 / 100, or 6 / 4 | Found the distance per minute (or the ratio) and stopped | 0 | 1 | 1 |
| 360 / 400, or 36 000 / 90 000 | Divided or multiplied 3600 by 10 (6000 by 15) in one step; the hour was never turned into minutes | 1 | 0 | 0 |

### Pair 3: у разів (inverted) + частини і ціле

**A.** «У першому мішку 36 кг картоплі, що в 3 рази більше, ніж у другому. Скільки кілограмів картоплі в обох мішках?»

```
I мішок — 36 кг, у 3 рази більше, ніж II
II мішок — ?
Разом — ?

Розв'язання
1) 36 : 3 = 12 (кг) — у другому мішку;
2) 36 + 12 = 48 (кг) — в обох мішках.
Відповідь: 48 кг.
```

**B.** «У першій коробці 32 олівці, що в 4 рази більше, ніж у другій. Скільки олівців в обох коробках?»

```
I коробка — 32 ол., у 4 рази більше, ніж II
II коробка — ?
Разом — ?

Розв'язання
1) 32 : 4 = 8 (ол.) — у другій коробці;
2) 32 + 8 = 40 (ол.) — в обох коробках.
Відповідь: 40 олівців.
```

**Tricky relation:** «…, що в 3 рази більше, ніж у другому». The first sack is the bigger one, so the second is 36 : 3. A good sign, though not required: she rewrites it in the short record as «II мішок — ?, у 3 рази менше».

| Her answer (A / B) | What happened | Asked | Plan | Decode |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 48 / 40 | Right | 1 | 2 | 1 |
| 144 / 160 | Multiplied for the second amount (108 / 128), then found the total | 1 | 2 | 0 |
| 12 / 8 | Found the second amount and stopped | 0 | 1 | 1 |
| 108 / 128 | Multiplied for the second amount and stopped | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| 39 / 36 | Added the two given numbers | 1 | 0 | 0 |

### Pair 4: на + на (inverted) + частини і ціле (the triangle)

**A.** «Сторона MN трикутника MNK дорівнює 6,3 см. Сторона NK на 2,6 см більша за MN, але на 4,8 см менша від MK. Знайди периметр трикутника.»

```
MN — 6,3 см
NK — ?, на 2,6 см більша, ніж MN
MK — ?, на 4,8 см більша, ніж NK
P — ?

Розв'язання
1) 6,3 + 2,6 = 8,9 (см) — довжина сторони NK;
2) 8,9 + 4,8 = 13,7 (см) — довжина сторони MK;
3) 6,3 + 8,9 + 13,7 = 28,9 (см) — периметр трикутника.
Відповідь: 28,9 см.
```

**B.** «Сторона DE трикутника DEF дорівнює 7,4 см. Сторона EF на 3,8 см більша за DE, але на 2,7 см менша від DF. Знайди периметр трикутника.»

```
DE — 7,4 см
EF — ?, на 3,8 см більша, ніж DE
DF — ?, на 2,7 см більша, ніж EF
P — ?

Розв'язання
1) 7,4 + 3,8 = 11,2 (см) — довжина сторони EF;
2) 11,2 + 2,7 = 13,9 (см) — довжина сторони DF;
3) 7,4 + 11,2 + 13,9 = 32,5 (см) — периметр трикутника.
Відповідь: 32,5 см.
```

**Tricky relation:** «NK … на 4,8 см менша від MK». MK is the bigger side, and it is compared with NK, not MN, so MK = NK + 4,8.

| Her answer (A / B) | What happened | Asked | Plan | Decode |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 28,9 / 32,5 | Right | 1 | 2 | 1 |
| 19,3 / 27,1 | Subtracted for the third side (4,1 / 8,5), then found the perimeter | 1 | 2 | 0 |
| 26,3 / 28,7 | Added 4,8 (2,7) to the first side instead of the second (11,1 / 10,1), then found the perimeter | 1 | 2 | 0 |
| 13,7 / 13,9 | **Check her steps.** Either she found both sides and stopped (Asked 0, Plan 1, Decode 1), or she added the three given numbers and called it the perimeter (Asked 1, Plan 0, Decode 0). The third side always equals the sum of the given numbers, so both routes give the same number. | — | — | — |
| 8,9 / 11,2 | Found the second side and stopped | 0 | 1 | 0 |

## Limits

- **The matching is a judgement, not a measurement.** Each pair shares its relations, wording and number kinds, but no one has checked that A and B are equally hard. Pair 2 B (100 · 15) has slightly heavier arithmetic than A (60 · 10). That touches only the Answer point.
- **Four of the six problem types are tested.** There's no дріб від числа or зближення relation, and no question-before-data or two-part question, although the problem set trains all of these. The checks stay at four problems so that they target the core: missing information, the unit change, inverted wording and a chain.
- **One learner, so no counterbalancing.** A always comes first, so any difference between A and B also includes whatever she learns at school in between.
- **Reserved stories.** The problem set must not reuse these stories or numbers: books on shelves, apples in baskets, a grandmother or a skier walking for an hour, potatoes in sacks, pencils in boxes, and triangles MNK and DEF. Slots 2.1, 2.3, 3.2 and 4.7 share their shapes.
