## Question

Which word-problem types go into the 10–30-problem set, in what order, and how many of each?

The ramp must:

- Start *easier* than the walking boy ("Хлопчик пройшов 3000 м за годину. Яку відстань він пройде за 20 хвилин?"), which she currently struggles with
- Reach at least the difficulty of the triangle (a perimeter built from a chain of "greater by" / "less by" comparisons)
- Use only numbers she already knows: whole numbers, common fractions, and simple decimals

Decide the levels, which problem types sit in each level, how many problems per level, and the total count.

Also decide, from [Which word-problem teaching methods work for kids her age?](01-teaching-methods.md):

- How many problem types she learns to name, and what each is called in Ukrainian
- Whether problems come grouped by type first and mixed later, or mixed from the start. In a randomized trial with 7th graders, mixed practice beat grouped practice. Naming the type is only real practice when the order of the problems doesn't give it away

Use the catalog in [How does Ukrainian 6th grade expect word problems to be solved on paper?](02-ukrainian-school-format.md). It has the school's problem types with real samples and their Ukrainian names, and which number kinds she has had. Her school follows **«Ліга крилатих»** (the parent, 2026-10-03). This is the grades 5–6 continuation of «Світ чекає крилатих». It has its own maths programme («Математика. Адаптаційний цикл. 5–6 класи», by О. В. Бугайова, approved 29.06.2022, 175 hours a year). Its topic order and textbook aren't published online, so use the НУШ picture from the summary by default. She is learning decimals for the first time this year (the parent, 2026-10-03). Her programme teaches them a year later than the НУШ model programmes, so:

- keep decimals simple, using only the operations she has covered
- expect percentages, fraction of a number and proportions to be still ahead of her

Before choosing number kinds, ask the parent:

- which decimal operations she has done so far, and whether she has done common fractions (a glance at her workbook will show this)
- whether her tasks say «Знайди» or «Знайдіть»

## Comments

### Resolution

Plan: [problem-set-plan.md](../assets/problem-set-plan.md). It has the six types, the number rules, the four levels and all 30 slots. Each slot lists its relations and a textbook shape to adapt.

**Facts from the parent (2026-10-03):**

- **Decimals.** She has done only + and −. In the parent's words, her difficulty is word problems "even with plain numbers".
- **Common fractions.** Covered, including fraction of a number.
- **Wording.** Her workbook says «Знайди», so problems use «ти».

**Answer.**

- **Six problem types, named per relation, not per problem.** The triangle is на + на (inverted) + частини і ціле. The names are:
  - На … більше / менше
  - У … разів більше / менше
  - Частини і ціле
  - Дріб від числа, which includes число за дробом
  - Три величини: motion, buying and work
  - Зближення / віддалення

  A joint-motion problem is a зближення relation plus a три величини one. Left out: joint work, two numbers from their sum and difference, averages, percentages and proportions.
- **Numbers.** Whole numbers by default. Decimals only with + and −, only in на and частини і ціле relations, and only in Levels 3–4. Fractions only in дріб від числа relations, with whole-number results. Divisions come out exact.
- **30 problems in four levels (7 / 8 / 8 / 7).** Each level adds one kind of difficulty:
  1. one relation
  2. two relations, with hidden information
  3. inverted wording
  4. chains of three or more

  Level 1 starts easier than the walking boy (one step, same units). The walking boy is slot 2.3, and the triangle closes the set as slot 4.7.
- **Order.** Level 1 is grouped, to introduce the names. Levels 2–4 are mixed: neighbouring problems never share the same set of types, and no title names a type.
- **Counts, by relation.** частини і ціле 18, три величини 15, на 14, у разів 5, дріб від числа 5, зближення 5. Seven problems carry inverted wording, all in Levels 3–4.

**Glossary.** [CONTEXT.md](../../../CONTEXT.md) gains Relation, Problem type (with the six names), Level and Inverted wording. Problem set is now 30 problems in four levels.
