## Question

How does the parent run the trial with her, so that the verdict gets what it needs: the parent's judgement of *does it help*, from what they know of her abilities before and after, and her own opinion of *is it interesting*?

Decide:

- **Days and minutes a day.** How many days the trial runs, how many sittings a week, and how long a sitting is. The studies behind the method ran 10–29 lessons, so a single pass through the problem set is a short dose. Products for her age plan sessions of 15–30 minutes.
- **Problems per sitting.** How many problems a sitting holds at each level, and whether a sitting ends on a problem count, on time, or at a natural break (a handover screen, the end of a level). Problems now run longer step by step: the triangle may take 8–11 minutes, so a sitting holds 1–2 Level 4 problems. A daily goal shouldn't count problems in a way that rushes her.
- **What the parent watches.** What they look for in her first tries, and at the solo try at 4.6–4.7 (the triangle among them), and what they write down each time. Nothing is solved unaided unless she chooses the solo try, so that choice and her homework are the natural moments to watch.
- **What the PoC shows the parent.** Whether «Для батьків» gets a short list of the problems where a plan line or a result had to be shown, and what else, if anything, it needs for the verdict. Dashboards are out of scope.
- **Her opinion for *is it interesting*.** When it's asked (during the trial, at the end, both), what is asked and in what words, and how her answers are kept.

What the PoC already records, on the device: hints, shown answers and slips per step. The fading adds plan-line picks, typed results with the sign behind a wrong one, direction-check answers, next-step picks and the solo-try choice. Apps that cap retries tell the teacher. The records stay on the iPad, read and reset under «Для батьків».

Practicalities to keep: on her iPad, the home-screen app keeps its progress apart from Safari, so she always opens it from the icon, not the link. Only one open problem resumes at a time.

From [How does the guidance fade across the problem set?](08-guidance-fading.md): a missed problem (a plan line or result had to be shown) comes back once at the end of its level, and from Level 2 she writes in a notebook beside the device, one step at a time. See [fading-schedule.md](../assets/fading-schedule.md).

Paper checks A and B were dropped on 2026-10-03 (see the map's Out of scope), so there's no before-and-after test: *does it help* rests on the parent's judgement.

Resolved when the parent has decided each part above. The resolution records the trial plan the parent follows, and anything the PoC must still build for it.

## Comments

### Note (2026-10-03, proposal)

An AFK proposal to grill from is in [trial-proposal.md](../assets/trial-proposal.md): a recommendation and alternatives for each part, with sources, plus what the verdict will need. Nothing in it is decided. In short:

- **Days and minutes**: 4 sittings a week, 15–25 minutes each. One pass with its repeats is 16–19 sittings, about 4–5 weeks. Then 1–2 free weeks with no reminders, with replays and the solo try open, and her homework watched.
- **Problems per sitting**: no count. She doesn't open a new problem after about 15 minutes, and each handover starts a sitting. The calendar is estimated, to be rechecked after week 1.
- **What the parent watches**: the parent sits beside her at the first sitting, the handovers and the solo tries, without teaching. They keep a one-line log per sitting, a short sheet when watching, and a weekly homework note made before helping.
- **What the PoC shows**: in «Для батьків», a «Де довелося показати» list of missed problems and how their repeats went, one line per solo try, minutes per attempt, and «Скопіювати записи».
- **Her opinion**: two open questions at the end of Levels 1–3 and a 10-minute chat after the free weeks, in her words. What she does is counted too (who starts a sitting, opening it in the free weeks). No rewards for sittings.
- **Verdict**: a "before" note and 2–3 homework photos now, and the same again after the free weeks.

The proposal leaves two questions for the parent: which weekdays, and whether they or another adult asks her opinion.

For when this resolves: if the parent keeps part 4, it becomes a small build ticket blocked by [Build the paper steps and the fading schedule](14-build-fading.md). The proposal also suggests that ticket 14 show its new records in words in «Для батьків». For the loop build: after 4.7 she can open any problem freely, with no reminders.

### Resolution

**Decided by the orchestrator on the parent's delegation** (2026-10-03: "everything up to you … make a research, check other saas, choose recommended option"). It adopts the recommendations in [trial-proposal.md](../assets/trial-proposal.md), which holds the reasons, the sources and the observation sheets. The parent can reopen this at any time.

- **Days and minutes.** 4 sittings a week on school days, 15–25 minutes each, on the same days every week; the parent picks which. One pass through the problem set with its repeats is about 16–19 sittings, 4–5 weeks. Then 1–2 free weeks with no schedule and no reminders: replays stay open with the solo try offered, and her homework is watched. Near the end the parent may offer once: «Хочеш спробувати якусь задачу сама?».
- **Problems per sitting.** No problem count. She doesn't open a new problem after about 15 minutes, and finishes the one that's open. Each handover (2.1, 2.5, 3.1, 4.1, 4.4, 4.6) starts a sitting. If she's tired, the sitting stops and the open problem resumes next time. The calendar in the proposal is an estimate, rechecked after week 1.
- **What the parent watches.** The parent sits beside her at the first sitting, each handover and each solo try (about 9 sittings), and otherwise stays within earshot and looks at her notebook afterwards. They don't teach. If she asks, they say «Спробуй — гра підкаже.» They keep a separate trial notebook for her write-ups and use the proposal's three sheets: a one-line log per sitting, a sheet when watching and at each solo try, and a weekly homework note made before helping.
- **What the PoC shows the parent.** «Для батьків» gets a «Де довелося показати» list (missed problems by level, what was shown, and how the repeat went), one line per solo try, minutes per finished attempt, and «Скопіювати записи» as a text export. That's built in [Show the trial records in «Для батьків»](18-trial-records.md). No charts, per-type scores or daily totals.
- **Her opinion for *is it interesting*.** The parent asks her (not another adult). Two open questions at the end of Levels 1, 2 and 3, and a 10-minute chat after the free weeks, at a relaxed moment. The questions are in the proposal, part 5. Her words go in the log as she says them, with the date. Her behaviour is counted too: who starts each sitting, asking for one more, opening it in the free weeks, choosing the solo try. No screen time or treats are traded for sittings.
- **For the verdict.** Before the trial, the parent writes a one-paragraph "before" note (template in the proposal) and photographs 2–3 recent homework word problems. After the free weeks, they do the same again under the same headings.

**Still the parent's:** picking the weekdays, writing the "before" note and photos before the trial starts, and running the trial with her.

**Passed on:** to [Build the paper steps and the fading schedule](14-build-fading.md), to show its new records in words. To the map's Building the PoC: after 4.7 she can open any problem freely, with no reminders, and a daily goal counts a sitting or one finished problem, not a number of problems.
